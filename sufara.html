This course follows the progression of the Sufara used in class: first recognize the Arabic letters and their written forms, then move gradually into reading signs and connected Qur'anic text.

1. Basic Sounds

Read each sound several times with your teacher or parent.

2. Long Vowel Practice

Practice the letter with ā, ī and ū.

3. Connecting Forms

Recognize the letter alone, at the start, in the middle and at the end.

4. Examples / Reading Practice

Read every row from right to left several times. The lesson grows from the letter and short combinations to connected words, following the Sufara lesson pattern.

5. Writing Practice

Trace the shapes with your finger, then write them on paper. This follows the Sufara pattern of “Let’s write together” and “Write over the examples.”
